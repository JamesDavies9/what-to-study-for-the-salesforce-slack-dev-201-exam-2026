<h1 data-end="49" data-section-id="d4qpkj" data-start="0" dir="auto" style="text-align: justify;">Salesforce Slack-Dev-201 Exam Preparation Guide</h1>

<p data-end="371" data-start="51" dir="auto" style="text-align: justify;">The Salesforce Slack-Dev-201 Exam&nbsp;is designed for developers who can build custom applications on the Slack platform and work with Slack APIs. Salesforce recommends that candidates have experience building a Slack app and around six months of experience working with Slack APIs. A strong preparation strategy should combine official learning material, hands-on development, and practice questions. Salesforce&#39;s own preparation trail covers Slack platform basics, app surfaces, workflows, APIs, security, and app distribution.</p>

<p data-end="823" data-start="695" dir="auto" style="text-align: justify;">Start by understanding how Slack apps are designed and how different platform components work together. Important areas include:</p>

<ul data-end="1174" data-start="825">
	<li data-end="863" data-section-id="r4wlb6" data-start="825" style="text-align: justify;">Slack apps and their core components</li>
	<li data-end="896" data-section-id="1n6uwq5" data-start="864" style="text-align: justify;">App Home, messages, and modals</li>
	<li data-end="934" data-section-id="qyk0tw" data-start="897" style="text-align: justify;">Block Kit and interactive workflows</li>
	<li data-end="965" data-section-id="136770a" data-start="935" style="text-align: justify;">Slack Web API and Events API</li>
	<li data-end="1004" data-section-id="14i8faw" data-start="966" style="text-align: justify;">Event handling and API communication</li>
	<li data-end="1032" data-section-id="mkrk2d" data-start="1005" style="text-align: justify;">OAuth, scopes, and tokens</li>
	<li data-end="1058" data-section-id="1b1dod7" data-start="1033" style="text-align: justify;">Security considerations</li>

	<li data-end="1174" data-section-id="1ok1yr6" data-start="1135" style="text-align: justify;">Designing scalable Slack applications</li>

<p data-end="1385" data-start="1176" dir="auto" style="text-align: justify;">Salesforce&#39;s official exam guide also recommends knowledge of JSON, HTTP-based APIs, WebSockets, and at least one programming language such as JavaScript, Python, or Java.</p>

<h2 data-end="1431" data-section-id="gth6mm" data-start="1387" dir="auto" style="text-align: justify;">How to Prepare for the&nbsp;Salesforce Slack-Dev-201 Exam</h2>

<p data-end="1668" data-start="1433" dir="auto" style="text-align: justify;">Don&#39;t rely only on reading documentation. Try to build and test small Slack applications while studying. For example, create a simple workflow, experiment with Block Kit components, work with API requests, and practice handling events.</p>

<p data-end="1696" data-start="1670" dir="auto" style="text-align: justify;">A useful study process is:</p>

<ol data-end="2051" data-start="1698">
	<li data-end="1737" data-section-id="wpdfyz" data-start="1698" style="text-align: justify;">Review the official exam objectives.</li>
	<li data-end="1787" data-section-id="1j3xo77" data-start="1738" style="text-align: justify;">Learn one Slack development concept at a time.</li>
	<li data-end="1823" data-section-id="1yb198l" data-start="1788" style="text-align: justify;">Build a small practical example.</li>
	<li data-end="1864" data-section-id="oqvfme" data-start="1824" style="text-align: justify;">Test how the API or workflow behaves.</li>
	<li data-end="1937" data-section-id="ku4n73" data-start="1865" style="text-align: justify;">Use Slack-Dev-201 exam questions&nbsp;to check your understanding.</li>
	<li data-end="1994" data-section-id="6lk3g5" data-start="1938" style="text-align: justify;">Review every mistake and return to the related topic.</li>
	<li data-end="2051" data-section-id="15lmnev" data-start="1995" style="text-align: justify;">Take mixed practice tests during your final revision.</li>
</ol>

<h2 data-end="2980" data-section-id="xm422z" data-start="2951" dir="auto" style="text-align: justify;">Final&nbsp;Salesforce Slack-Dev-201 Exam Preparation Strategy</h2>

<p data-end="3256" data-start="2982" dir="auto" style="text-align: justify;">The best way to structure your preparation is to combine concept learning + hands-on development + practice questions + mistake review.
